# Dawnwatch Governance

## Core rule

Dawnwatch exists to warn people using evidence, not to automate accusations.

## Publication controls

A public `HIGH RISK` or `CRITICAL WARNING` case should normally require:
1. sufficiently resolved entity identity;
2. attributable evidence;
3. either Tier A/B support for a serious indicator or multiple independent corroborating sources;
4. clear separation of fact, claim, allegation, and inference;
5. human-readable rationale;
6. review of named-person relationships;
7. a correction/right-of-reply mechanism.

## Right of reply

An affected entity may provide:
- licensing evidence;
- identity corrections;
- corporate records;
- underlying custody/broker evidence;
- explanations of disputed claims;
- legal outcomes.

A reply does not automatically remove indicators; it becomes evidence that must be assessed.

## Prohibited product behavior

Dawnwatch should not become:
- a pay-to-remove reputation service;
- an automated blacklist without appeal;
- a rumor amplifier;
- a private dossier marketplace;
- a generalized people-scoring system;
- a platform for harassment or brigading.

Commercial relationships must never alter risk classification.
