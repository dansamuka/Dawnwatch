**Purpose:** Test how early Dawnwatch would have raised concern using only evidence publicly available at each historical date.

## Lead-time result

The seeded historical record currently produces:

> **ELEVATED CAUTION 45 days before the reported 5 September 2026 account freeze.**

This is a deliberately conservative benchmark. Later reporting about withdrawal failure, additional-payment demands, PCEX, and Apollo is **not allowed to leak backward** into the July replay.

## Why this matters

A post-collapse system can look artificially accurate if it uses facts that were discovered only after people lost money. Dawnwatch therefore stores two clocks:

1. when the underlying event allegedly or reportedly occurred; and
2. when evidence of it first became available to the system.

Historical replay uses the second clock.

## Current limitation

This fixture does **not yet reconstruct the full pre-July social footprint**. It therefore should not be interpreted as the earliest date Dawnwatch ultimately could have detected QVSE.

P1 research should add dated, independently sourced evidence for:

- initial Kenya recruitment;
- domain/company creation;
- advertised returns;
- referral mechanics;
- early social-media skepticism;
- founder-identity anomalies;
- payment rails;
- any publicly visible licensing claims;
- weekend/market-hours trading anomalies;
- complaint language before the freeze.

As these are added with accurate first-publication dates, the replay can test whether Dawnwatch would have reached HIGH RISK or CRITICAL WARNING earlier than the current benchmark.
